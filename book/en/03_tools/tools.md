# Tools

Choose by task, traceability, institutional access, export and preservation. Test a small dataset before entering the whole review.

| Need | Official options | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| References and duplicates | [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/) | Review merges; retain provenance |
| Screening and protocol | [Rayyan](https://www.rayyan.ai/), [Parsifal](https://parsif.al/), [CADIMA](https://www.cadima.info/), [Covidence](https://www.covidence.org/) | Check roles, independence, disagreements and export |
| Active learning | [ASReview](https://asreview.nl/) | Validate ranking, stopping and auditing |
| Extraction | [EPPI-Reviewer](https://eppi.ioe.ac.uk/), [JBI SUMARI](https://sumari.jbi.global/) | Or a spreadsheet: defined fields and source pages |
| Citation discovery | [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/), [ResearchRabbit](https://www.researchrabbit.ai/), [Connected Papers](https://www.connectedpapers.com/), [Litmaps](https://www.litmaps.com/) | Log steps, coverage and date |
| AI assistance | [Elicit](https://elicit.com/), [Scite](https://scite.ai/), [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google/) | Verify documents and each extracted item |
| Bibliometrics | [VOSviewer](https://www.vosviewer.com/), [bibliometrix](https://www.bibliometrix.org/) | Does not replace critical synthesis |
| Visualisation | [PRISMA2020](https://eshackathon.org/projects/prisma2020/) | R, Python or a spreadsheet for other plots |

## Active learning and stopping rules

Published savings apply to particular datasets and configurations; they do not guarantee retrieval in your review {cite:p}`vandeschoot2021`. Simulation on labelled data is not validation of a new review. Ranking records and leaving records unread are different decisions.

A run of 50–100 irrelevant records does not establish that no relevant studies remain. Justify stopping, auditing and residual risk; report unevaluated records and how they were handled. Volume alone does not determine the need for active learning.

## Conditions to verify

Free plans, prices, versions and limits change. Consult official links and log date, version or plan, cost and applicable restrictions. Do not budget from historical figures. Check decision independence, role-based access, privacy and disagreement exports. Periodically preserve open-format copies of references, decisions, extraction and the data dictionary. The [Systematic Review Toolbox](http://systematicreviewtools.com/) helps locate alternatives by phase.
